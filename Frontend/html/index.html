Tanker Ship

Large vessels designed to transport liquid bulk cargo such as crude oil, petroleum products, or chemicals. They are critical to global energy supply chains and optimized for safe, high-volume transport across long distances.

Fishing Trawler

Fishing trawlers feed the seafood supply chain by harvesting fish at scale. They connect ocean resources to processing plants and distribution networks. Their role highlights primary resource extraction, where catch volumes, fuel use, and route efficiency influence both sustainability and market availability

Surfer Boat

Surfer boats, though smaller, play a role in last-mile maritime logistics. They are agile vessels used for coastal transport, recreation, and sometimes patrol or inspection. In the supply chain, they symbolize short-distance, flexible operations, bridging gaps between ports, offshore facilities, and local communities

Oil Service Boat

Oil service boats are critical support vessels for offshore drilling platforms. They deliver crew, spare parts, and equipment, ensuring uninterrupted energy production. Within the supply chain, they represent support and maintenance logistics, enabling upstream operations to function smoothly and keeping the energy flow consistent
